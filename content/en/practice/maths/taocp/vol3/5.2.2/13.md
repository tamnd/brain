[Section 5.2.2: Sorting by Exchanging](../)

**Exercise 13.** [*10*] Is Batcher's method a stable sorting algorithm?

**Verified:** no  
**Solve time:** 4m16s  

---

Yes, Batcher's merge exchange sort is stable. Every compare-exchange operation in the algorithm is defined to swap two records only when their keys are in strictly decreasing order (i.e., \(K_i > K_j\)). Equal keys are therefore never interchanged directly. Moreover, because all operations are of this form, two equal keys can never cross each other indirectly: any sequence of such stable comparators preserves the original relative order of equal elements. Hence the algorithm is stable. ∎
